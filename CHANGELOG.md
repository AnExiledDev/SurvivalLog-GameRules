# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- Food: raw food sickness chance, and Appetite Loss from eating the same food can be turned off, made shorter or longer, and made milder or harsher.
- Mold: Disinfectant Spray radius and cost, how fast moldy food spoils, how often mold spreads, how many items it spreads to, and how many items start moldy.
- Rats: rat visit chance, and how long a rat stays before it escapes.
- Sickness: turn off the Outbreak crisis or only its worsening, and set the Wind Chill and High Temperature chances in a cold wave.
- Power: how much power devices use, a later city power cut or none at all, and no Blackout panic.
- House: no heavy rain leaks.
- Cold: how cold cold waves are, and extra levels of Personal Cold Resistance.
- Stray zombies: turn them off, or set their start day, the time between visits, visits per day, how many at once, how often a crawler leads them, and what they drop.
- Hordes: keep story hordes at full strength, and scale horde loot.
- Companions: the neighbour and your companion can be kept from dying, or given a later rescue deadline.
- Strangers: how much trust strangers you support lose, how often they suffer mishaps, and which mishaps can happen.
- How often new commissions and Aid Platform requests arrive.
- How often your character thinks out loud, morale from music and perfect harvests, the vase morale limit, full crafting proficiency from lower-level recipes, and how far away you can pick up items.

## 0.4.2

- With Start with all areas open, a Repair Stairs task or other area task that was already on the task list (from a saved run) is ended once its area is open.

- After a game update, a hook that can no longer be placed is logged as an error, so the Mod Manager can mark the mod as maybe needing an update.

## 0.4.1

- Start with all areas open now opens the areas when the disaster hits, not before. Before the disaster the house is a separate map with no way onto the other floors, so opened floors could not be reached there. Building spots on those floors are no longer opened before the disaster.

## 0.4.0

- The Mods screen links this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, settings that only matter while another setting is on stay hidden until it is.
- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once, except NoRestartLimit, BatteryMultiplier, StartWithAllAreasOpen and the Weather settings, which take effect at the next game start.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings changed on the Mods screen take effect without a restart; without it, at the next game start.

## 0.3.0

- Start with all areas open: the second floor and basement, or the Warehouse Manager's second floor and locked rooms, are open from the start and can be built on before the disaster. Works on a save already under way. The events that would open them are skipped.

Off by default.

## 0.2.0

- Battery multiplier: batteries hold 0.1x to 10x the game's power.
- Turn off the door-smasher ("Midnight Door Pounding") and the debt collector (the loan shark's visits and texts).
- Random weather: roll the weather from chances you set, with a seed. Cold waves and fixed story days keep the game's weather.

Every new setting defaults to the game's own rule.

## 0.1.0

First version. No restart limit, stop mold spreading, a solar multiplier and solar power in bad weather, food spoilage speed, and whole-floor heat sharing from Whole Floor Climate. Every setting defaults to the game's own rule.
