# Changelog

## 0.7.1

- **Warning:** install Power Manager to keep your power settings. The solar, battery, device power, power cut and Blackout panic settings are no longer in Game Rules. Power Manager carries over your values the first time it starts.
- With Start with all areas open, the Warehouse Manager can now walk into the two side rooms, cold storage and the garage. Their doors stayed shut before, with no way to unlock them. A run that already has the areas open loses those doors the next time it loads.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.7.0

- The solar, battery, device power, power cut and Blackout panic settings moved to the new Power Manager mod. Power Manager carries over your values the first time it starts.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.6.3

- Less stutter the first time shared device heat warms a zone in a session: Game Rules now reads the zones once while the save loads.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.6.2

- Less stutter with shared device heat, solar panel settings and the other rules turned on.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.6.1

- Less stutter with shared device heat on: each zone's heat is read from the game once per frame instead of dozens of times, and the once-a-second checks no longer all run on the same frame.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.6.0

- Neighbour and companion: how fast they drop a condition level, how long the basket takes to come back, the neighbour's crisis chance, the affinity lost for disliked food, how many times they can pull through near death, and how many gifts come back in the basket.

## 0.5.0

- Food: raw food sickness chance, and Appetite Loss from eating the same food can be turned off, made shorter or longer, and made milder or harsher.
- Mold: Disinfectant Spray radius and cost, how fast moldy food spoils, how often mold spreads, how many items it spreads to, and how many items start moldy.
- Rats: rat visit chance, and how long a rat stays before it escapes.
- Sickness: turn off the Outbreak crisis or only its worsening, and set the Wind Chill and High Temperature chances in a cold wave.
- Power: how much power devices use, a later city power cut or none at all, and no Blackout panic.
- House: no heavy rain leaks.
- Cold: how cold cold waves are, and extra levels of Personal Cold Resistance.
- Stray zombies: turn them off, or set their start day, the time between visits, visits per day, how many at once, how often a crawler leads them, and what they drop.
- Hordes: keep story hordes at full strength, and scale horde loot.
- Companions: the neighbour and your companion can be kept from dying, or given a later rescue deadline.
- Strangers: how much trust strangers you support lose, how often they suffer mishaps, and which mishaps can happen.
- How often new commissions and Aid Platform requests arrive.
- How often your character thinks out loud, morale from music and perfect harvests, the vase morale limit, full crafting proficiency from lower-level recipes, and how far away you can pick up items.

## 0.4.2

- With Start with all areas open, a Repair Stairs task or other area task that was already on the task list (from a saved run) is ended once its area is open.
- After a game update, Mod Manager can tell you if this mod may need an update.

## 0.4.1

- Start with all areas open now opens the areas when the disaster hits, not before. Before the disaster the house is a separate map with no way onto the other floors, so opened floors could not be reached there. Building spots on those floors are no longer opened before the disaster.

## 0.4.0

- Mod Manager's Mods screen links to this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, a setting that only matters while another one is on stays hidden until that one is on.
- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once, except NoRestartLimit, BatteryMultiplier, StartWithAllAreasOpen and the Weather settings, which take effect at the next game start.
- Mod Manager's Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings you change on the Mods screen take effect right away. Without it, they take effect the next time you start the game.

## 0.3.0

- Start with all areas open: the second floor and basement, or the Warehouse Manager's second floor and locked rooms, are open from the start and can be built on before the disaster. Works on a save already under way. The events that would open them are skipped. Off by default.

## 0.2.0

- Battery multiplier: batteries hold 0.1x to 10x the game's power.
- Turn off the door-smasher ("Midnight Door Pounding") and the debt collector (the loan shark's visits and texts).
- Random weather: roll the weather from chances you set, with a seed. Cold waves and fixed story days keep the game's weather.
- Every new setting defaults to the game's own rule.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- No restart limit.
- Mold can be stopped from spreading.
- A solar multiplier, and solar power in bad weather.
- Food spoilage speed.
- Heat shared across a whole floor, from Whole Floor Climate.
- Every setting defaults to the game's own rule.
